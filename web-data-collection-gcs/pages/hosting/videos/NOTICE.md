**Where they came from.** These are widely redistributed clips; we did not record
them and cannot identify the specific upload each file was taken from. The underlying
events are documented at:

- *Coach Carter* — <https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0393162/>
- The 2022 Oscars incident — <https://www.oscars.org/oscars/ceremonies/2022>
- The Oval Office meeting, 2025-02-28 — <https://www.c-span.org/>

We previously described the third clip as "public domain / C-SPAN". That was wrong
and has been withdrawn. C-SPAN places only House and Senate floor proceedings in the
public domain; its coverage of other federal government events is its own, licensed
for non-commercial use on condition that C-SPAN is attributed on screen. Our file
carries no C-SPAN logo and is downscaled, so we cannot establish that C-SPAN
distributed it and do not rely on that permission.

**Basis for inclusion.** Fair use, for non-commercial academic research. The clips
serve as standardized emotional stimuli; the purpose is scientific measurement rather
than entertainment, each is a short low-resolution excerpt of a much longer work, and
none substitutes for the original. They are shipped rather than linked because the
study is not replicable without the exact stimuli. See the "Video Copyright and
Licensing" section of `web-data-collection-gcs/README.md`.

**If you deploy this study**, you may prefer to substitute your own clips rather than
redistribute these. See "Replacing Videos" in that README.

**If you are a rights holder** and believe this use does not qualify as fair use, open
an issue on this repository and we will remove the file.
